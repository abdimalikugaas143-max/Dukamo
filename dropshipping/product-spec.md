## Page copy

### Hero subtitle (under title)
> Real rain. Real calm. The cloud-shaped diffuser that turns any room into a
> cozy mood — with a working water-drip and seven night-light colors.

### Bullet benefits (above the fold)
- **Watch it rain indoors.** Real water drips from the cloud — mesmerizing for
  hours, especially in the dark.
- **Aromatherapy + humidifier in one.** Add a few drops of essential oil for
  cool mist that calms tired eyes and dry air.
- **Seven ambient colors.** Tap to cycle: warm white, sunset orange, pink,
  purple, blue, green, or color-cycle for a slow rainbow.
- **Auto shut-off + USB powered.** Safe to leave on overnight; plugs into any
  USB outlet or power bank.

### How it works (3 steps with icons)
1. Fill the base tank with water (and 2–3 drops of essential oil, optional).
2. Hang the cloud above the tank — magnets hold it in place.
3. Tap the base once for mist, again for the night light.

### FAQ (collapsible on the page)

**How loud is it?**
Whisper-quiet — about as loud as a quiet refrigerator. Safe for sleep.

**How long does one tank last?**
6–8 hours of continuous mist and rain.

**Does it really make rain?**
Yes — water cycles up into the cloud and drips down. It's the whole point.

**What essential oils work?**
Any water-soluble essential oil. Lavender, eucalyptus, and orange are the
customer favorites.

**How fast does it ship?**
Free US shipping. Delivery in 5–9 business days from our US warehouse.

**What's the return policy?**
